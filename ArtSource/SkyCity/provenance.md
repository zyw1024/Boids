# Sky City asset provenance

Created locally for this scene in September 2026. Architecture, vegetation and birds are generated by the checked-in Blender Python authoring script. No external model library, downloaded environment pack, or third-party city model is used.

Three images were generated through Codex's `image_gen` tool. The concept is reference-only. Only the limestone image remains an active Unity surface asset. The original cloud matte is retained as an unused earlier study; current clouds are ray-marched 3D density, generated locally with a compute shader. The model and texture roles are deliberately different and are recorded here to make the pipeline reproducible.

## SkyCity_Concept.png — direction reference

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art direction reference for a beautiful real-time 3D game scene, landscape 3:2. Create an exquisitely composed SKY CITY, a poetic inhabited floating garden above a sea of clouds. A magnificent ivory limestone palace on a craggy floating island occupies the left-middle of the frame: distinct graceful verdigris copper domes, asymmetrically grouped slender campanile towers, real open arcaded loggias, tiered garden terraces, slim cypress trees, warm ochre roofs, tiny brass details, cascading ivy. Architectural beauty and readable crafted forms, a believable old city with a carefully designed silhouette, not a jumble of randomly repeated towers. A delicate arched aqueduct bridge sweeps toward a smaller floating pavilion in the right middle distance, creating beautiful large negative spaces. Floating rock undersides have layered angular stone, moss and fine roots, partly disappearing into luminous clouds. A graceful flock of small ivory and slate swallows arcs from the lower-left foreground through the open central-right sky and toward the distance; a few larger foreground birds have elegant tapered wings and long forked tails. Morning light enters from upper right, warm apricot and old gold on limestone, cool soft lavender-blue shadows, pale blue upper sky, atmospheric perspective. Sculptural pearly cumulus billows make a rich lower foreground and frame the edges, with luminous mist between islands. Cinematic stylized 3D environment art with the visual refinement of a hand-painted animated feature; soft brush-like material color variation on fully dimensional modeled architecture, restrained sophisticated colors, spatial depth, immaculate art direction. The main palace and bird flight are the focus. No text, no UI, no border, no people, no robots, no flying ships. Avoid generic low-poly blocks, empty flat backgrounds, excessive clutter, overly saturated blue, tiny unreadable architecture. This image is a reference, not a background to be pasted into the game.

## WeatheredLimestone.png — cliff albedo

Location: `Sky_City_Project/Assets/SkyCity/Content/Shared/Textures/WeatheredLimestone.png`.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless square albedo material texture for the genuinely modeled vertical limestone cliffs beneath an artistic floating sky city. Full-bleed evenly illuminated flat surface, no scenery, no island silhouette, no sky, no perspective, no cast shadows, no baked directional highlights. Weathered warm pale-grey limestone, cool muted slate crevices, subtle ochre mineral staining, finely fractured irregular stone plates and long discontinuous vertical hairline cracks, small stratification, a few sparse olive moss traces. Sophisticated hand-painted 3D game material with tactile natural detail at several scales, muted harmonious colors, medium luminance. Painterly stone surface, no obvious repeated pattern, no over-sharpening, no metallic sheen, no harsh pure black fissures. 2048x2048, tileable horizontally and vertically. The texture must be useful on actual three-dimensional craggy rock surfaces rather than depict a scene. No text.

## DawnCloudscape.png — unused earlier atmosphere study

Location: `Sky_City_Project/Assets/SkyCity/Content/Shared/Textures/DawnCloudscape.png`. Not bound by the current sky shader or scene builder. It contains only natural sky and clouds. It is not the reference concept, and contains none of the architecture or birds rendered in the game.

Generation prompt:

> Create a finished production sky background texture for a fixed-camera 3D fantasy game, landscape 3:2, preferably 1536x1024 or larger. This is ONLY the natural SKY AND CLOUD SEA: absolutely NO architecture, NO buildings, NO towers, NO land or floating islands, NO birds or animals, NO text. Painterly cinematic animated-feature beauty, poetic radiant morning above an immense sea of luminous cumulus. Camera looks horizontally and slightly down from high above the clouds. Composition: leave generous calm open pale cornflower and periwinkle blue sky through the center and upper middle. Richly sculptural towering cloud formations frame the extreme upper-left edge and upper-right edge, then dissolve into fine feathery cirrus around the upper-right golden sun glow, which is just outside the frame. From approximately the lower half downwards, a vast continuous sea of billowing clouds recedes into atmospheric depth: very tiny detailed distant cloud turrets at the horizon, medium clouds in the middle distance, large exquisite soft cloud curls in the lower foreground. Every cloud has detailed organic lobes at many scales, highly subtle wisps and filaments, NOT detached repeated cotton balls. Glowing warm creamy ivory, pale apricot, rose-gold silver linings on upper-right facing ridges, lavender blue and muted lilac in deep translucent undersides. The lower third should be a wonderful tapestry of warm luminous cloud tops with a winding shadowed valley through the lower center, suggesting immense height and movement. Strong sense of warm grazing sunlight, soft volumetric scattering, gentle rays from the far upper right, sophisticated harmonious restrained colors, inviting dreamlike atmosphere. High quality sky matte painting usable behind genuinely 3D modeled foreground architecture, painterly but materially believable, no black outlines, no flat cartoon blobs, no ground, no pasted silhouettes, no giant sun disk. The result must itself be a beautiful atmospheric natural skyscape. Keep the central-left middle zone relatively calm to frame the game's separately rendered ivory palace.

The prompts state desired resolution and tiling; those are authoring requests, not claims that every generated pixel meets a formal seamlessness test. Actual previews are rendered and reviewed in Unity.
